# PixelFocus Diamond V1

![Preview](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/7ad832bdddfec5f750f18a058dbf7051504e5ea5.png)

PixelFocus Diamond V1 is a custom Minecraft crosshair featuring a clean and symmetrical diamond design. Created for players who want a minimalist yet highly visible aiming point, this crosshair provides excellent visual clarity while maintaining an unobtrusive appearance on screen.

## Features

- Clean diamond-shaped design
- Easy to see on most backgrounds
- Minimal distraction during gameplay
- Suitable for PvP, survival, creative, and exploration
- Lightweight and simple visual style
- Designed to improve focus and aiming consistency

## Perfect For

- Competitive PvP players
- Survival world adventures
- Building and precision placement
- Parkour challenges
- General everyday Minecraft gameplay

## Compatibility

Works with Minecraft resource packs that support custom crosshair textures. Simply install the pack and enjoy a refreshed aiming experience.

## Why Choose PixelFocus Diamond V1?

Unlike overly complex crosshairs, PixelFocus Diamond V1 keeps the center of your screen clear while providing a distinct shape that is easy to locate instantly. The balanced diamond design offers a modern look and a comfortable gameplay experience across different Minecraft activities.

Upgrade your crosshair with a clean, focused, and practical design built for Minecraft players who value clarity and precision.
